Welcome to a whole new year of English

One school year, week by week: you’ll practise speaking, writing, listening and grammar through stories, games and real-life topics — from a seaside town to helping out in your community. This page is your map for the whole year, and your archive for catching up, practising and finding things again.

// why are we learning this?

English opens doors everywhere — today and in your future.

A world language

English lets you talk to people, watch films, play games and read stories from all over the world — without a translation.

New stories every unit

You’ll follow places and characters through their everyday adventures — and use them to practise real English along the way.

Confidence to speak up

Lots of speaking practice — with a partner, in games and in class — so English starts to feel natural, not scary.

For fast finishers

Extra challenges are always around the corner if something feels easy — there’s always a next step.

How this page works

How confident do you feel? Tap the stars — perfect for catching up or revising before a test. Saved on your device.

Around the English-speaking world — and beyond

This year you’ll travel — in English — from the outback of Australia to the streets of Mumbai and a part-time job in London, before looking beyond the UK to the wider English-speaking world. This page is your map for the whole year, and your archive for catching up, practising and finding things again.

// why are we learning this?

English gets more real every year — this is where it counts.

One world, many Englishes

Australia, India, the UK — you’ll see how English sounds and works differently around the globe, and why that matters.

Real-life skills

Write a CV, prepare for a job interview, summarise an article — skills you’ll actually use outside the classroom.

Ready for exams

Structured Exam Prep units build listening, reading, writing, speaking and mediation — step by step, not last-minute.

For fast finishers

Extra grammar and discussion challenges are always ready if a topic feels easy.

How this page works

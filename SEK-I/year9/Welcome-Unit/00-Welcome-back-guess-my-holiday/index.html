Guess My Holiday

A mystery photo guessing game, moderated by you, in English.

Before class, everyone secretly sent in one holiday photo. Nobody knows whose photo is whose yet, so let's find out together!

How to play

Five simple steps

  1. One mystery photo appears on the screen, no names, just the picture.
  2. In small groups, guess together: Where was this taken? Who do you think it is?
  3. Use English guessing language, see the sentence starters on the next slide.
  4. The photographer reveals the destination and shares one highlight from the trip.
  5. Next photo, same again, until everyone's holiday has been guessed.

Guessing language

Sentence starters

Making a guess

“I think this is …”

“It might be … because …”

“This looks like … to me.”

“My guess would be …”

Reasoning from clues

“Judging by the …, I’d say …”

“The … suggests that …”

“You can tell from the … that …”

Asking for more

“Was it as … as it looks?”

“What was the best part?”

“Did anything go wrong?”

EXTRA · Welcome-Unit Group jigsaw 10 + 10 + 5×5 minutes

Going Digital, Staying Healthy

We are becoming a tablet class. Before we start, let’s look at how other countries and researchers are handling phones, social media and AI — then decide, together, our own goals and rules for using our tablets and our own devices respectfully and in a healthy way.

Why this jigsaw?

Four short reasons

1

Real evidence, not just opinions

Belgium, Australia and Denmark have all made real decisions about phones, social media and screens — based on real research.

2

English in action

Every source you read and every source you cite in your presentation is genuine, current, English-language material.

3

Your voice counts

You are not just reading about other people’s rules — you are writing your own, for our tablet class.

4

One puzzle, five pieces

No single group has the full picture. You need all five expert topics to make a good decision — that’s the point of a jigsaw.

How the jigsaw works

Your tasks, step by step

Same three phases as the diagram above

1) Expert group

10 MIN
  1. Open your topic card above — everyone with the same topic meets up.
  2. Read the key facts and quotes together. Open at least one linked source.
  3. Fill in “Your expert notes” on the page: 3 facts + 1 question.
  4. Agree as a group: how will each of you explain this in under 60 seconds?

2) Home group

10 MIN
  1. Form a new group with one expert from each of the five topics.
  2. Each expert explains their topic in under 60 seconds — the others listen and can ask one question.
  3. Together, fill in the worksheet below: your goals and your rules.
  4. Choose who presents which part in Phase 3.

3) Presentation

5 MIN × GROUP
  • Name at least one fact from each of the five expert topics.
  • Present your group’s top 2–3 goals and top 2–3 rules.
  • Explain why — connect back to what your experts found out.
  • Listen to the other groups: which ideas do we want to combine into our real class rules?
